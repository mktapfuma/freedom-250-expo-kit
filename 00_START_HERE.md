## The print sizes — give the printer these exactly

| piece | print size |
|---|---|
| Pull-up banner | **850 × 2000 mm** |
| Table banner | **1000 × 2000 mm** |
| Backdrop | **2000 × 3000 mm** |
| Flyer | **A5 (148 × 210 mm)** — front and back |
| Card | **A6 (105 × 148 mm)** |

**Ask the printer for full bleed, no scaling.** A printer who scales to fit shrinks the
type and clips the edge. The PDFs carry the physical size on their own; if you send a
PNG, send these numbers with it.

**If only one thing gets printed, print the pull-up.** It is what a visitor sees from
across the room and it carries all three QR codes.

## The three rules on the floor

1. **Open with the browser, not a slide deck** — the live site, the live blog, and a
   ninety-second walkthrough.
2. **Nobody commits to anything.** No price, no rate, no package, no discount, no
   contract, no promise of a date. The money envelope on the day is empty.
3. **Write down what the visitor actually asked for, in their words**, and who decides.
