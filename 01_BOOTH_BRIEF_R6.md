## 3 · The hard questions, and the honest answers

**"Do you operate in Zimbabwe? Do you have clients here?"**
> "Not yet — that's exactly why I'm here. I'm a Zimbabwean who's been building from
> outside the country, and I'm looking to work with people here. What I'm showing you is
> the work I actually ship, and I'd rather talk about what you'd want built than claim a
> footprint I don't have."

**"What does it cost?"**
> "It depends entirely on scope, and I'd want to understand the problem before I price
> anything. Can I take a few details and come back to you with something concrete?"
*(Do not quote a number. Do not quote a rate card. Do not offer a discount. Price is the
Founder's decision and no approved service catalogue exists.)*

**"Who are your clients / have you worked with X?"**
> "I'll show you the work and the writing instead of names — most of what I publish is
> under my own name because I can't name my clients without their permission."
*(If asked for a reference: take it as a lead and pass it to the Founder. Do not name a
client, a partner, or an employer.)*

**"Do you work with Azure / Grafana / Cloudflare / Dynatrace?"** *(they are on our own
site's "tools I work with" line, and the blog carries a vendor topic label)*
> "Those are tools I work with — not partners, and not a contract. I'd rather show you
> the work than the logo wall."
*(Never say partner, sponsor, reseller, endorsed or certified by. No vendor is a partner
of ours and nothing we show may imply one.)*

## 4 · What must not be said, and what must not be on screen

- **No price, rate, package, discount or "we can do it for X".** Price is the Founder's
  decision and no approved service catalogue exists.
- **No claim of Zimbabwe presence, delivery, local staff, local clients or certification.**
  Nothing of the kind is on record.
- **No claim about any other exhibitor, sponsor or organiser** — including who else is
  exhibiting and whether the room is full.
- **No promise of a date** — no "we can deliver this by December".
- **No partnership, sponsorship or press conversation** unless the Founder has said
  beforehand who holds them. The Founder holds them. §3 has the line.
- **No credential, secret, token, tenant id, account id, customer name or internal URL** —
  on screen, in the recording, or in what you say.

> **§4, the no-credentials rule, as ruled.** No credentials, tokens, tenant or account
> identifiers, customer names, dashboards with identifying data, internal URLs, or client
> material — on screen or in the recording. No organisation chart or team diagram on the
> laptop either; it carries our own people's names. The booth laptop runs a clean browser
> profile with only the three public links and the video file. If a screen would need a
> secret to look impressive, we do not show it.

**Never open on the booth laptop:** the Dynatrace lab host, any IAM or account-management
route, any pipeline page or log, the `ktasolutions/platform` repository or its history, any
credential drawer or `.env`, the client build, the registry or database consoles, the
Grafana / Kubernetes / k3s dashboards, this channel, or another exhibitor's screen.
**The one-line gate before the laptop opens: *if it is not one of the three public URLs or
the recorded file, it does not open.***

> **§4, NEW in R4 — the cash rule.** Nothing is bought on the floor. No printing, no
>tablecloth, no transport, no lunch, no "let me just get that for us" — nothing, without

> it goes in the sheet as an offer, not as a transaction. Any money we spend is prepaid

## 6 · NEW in R4 — the three of you, and who does what

**Angeline — the lead.** Speaks. Runs the demo. Holds the pitch and the recording remote.
Decides in the moment what to show. Owns the quality of every conversation. **The Founder

**Juliana — support.** Keeps the sheet and writes every lead down in the visitor's own

**Vimbai — support.** Added by the Founder on 30 October 2026 (same event as above).
His line is the one that keeps three people from talking over each other: **he holds the
second conversation** when the table is busy, so a visitor never stands waiting while the
lead is mid-pitch, and he brings that visitor into the conversation at the point where the
lead hands over. He writes down anything he takes on the sheet. He handles the bag, water
and cables with Juliana. **He photographs — with permission, per §7 — and he does not
speak for the company.**
**Between you, in every conversation:** one person talks and one person writes. Nobody
loses a lead because two people were talking. **With three, the rule is: Angeline or
Vimbai talks, Juliana or Vimbai writes — never both at once on the same conversation.**

**Escalation:** the Founder is reachable from **09:00 to 13:00** on the number in §0. If
the venue says we may not have the space we expected, **call the Founder before
adapting** — do not renegotiate with the organiser on our own.

## 8 · The three questions to ask every one

1. "What are you trying to build or fix at the moment?"
2. "Who else is involved in the decision?"
3. "What would have to be true for you to move on this in the next month?"

Write their answers **in their words**. A lead is qualified when we can write what they
want built and who decides. Everything else is a contact.
