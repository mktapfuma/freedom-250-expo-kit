The honest answers

The questions every booth gets, and the answers we give. No price, no claims we cannot evidence, no partner logos.

"Do you operate in Zimbabwe? Do you have clients here?"

Not yet - that is exactly why I am here. I am a Zimbabwean building from outside the country, and I am looking to work with people here. What I show you is the work I actually ship, rather than a footprint I do not have.

"What does it cost?"

It depends entirely on scope, and I want to understand the problem before I price anything. Take a few details and I will come back with something concrete. No rate card on this page, on purpose.

"Who are your clients? Can you name one?"

I show the work and the writing instead of names. Most of what I publish is under my own name, because I cannot name clients without their permission.

"Do you work with Azure / Grafana / Cloudflare / Dynatrace?"

Those are tools I work with - not partners, and not a contract. I would rather show you the work than a logo wall.

"Are you looking for sponsors, partners or investment?"

That is our founder's conversation and he comes to you himself. May I take your name and what you are looking for so he comes prepared?

"What must not be on this page?"

No price, rate, package or discount. No claim of a local presence, local staff or local clients. No vendor, client or partner logo. The full booth brief is one click away, below.
